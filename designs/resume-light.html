Story

I became a software engineer the long way around — starting with curiosity about how the machines underneath everything actually work, and staying because the questions kept getting more interesting. I've spent most of my career on the backend, building tools, services, and small infrastructures that other people's work depends on.

What I care about is legibility: systems that are small enough to hold in your head, that fail in understandable ways, and that leave room for the next person to change their mind. I'm happiest at the seams between teams and between systems, where the work is as much writing as it is coding.
